# The rules, restated every time

<div class="grid grid-cols-3 gap-5 mt-8">

<div class="rounded-xl bg-gradient-to-br from-rose-500 to-rose-700 text-white p-6 shadow-md">
<div class="text-3xl font-bold opacity-30 mb-1">01</div>
<div class="font-bold text-lg mb-2">Nothing confidential</div>
<div class="text-sm opacity-90">No patient data, no unpublished data, no credentials, no identifiable text. Ever, in any prompt.</div>
</div>

<div class="rounded-xl bg-gradient-to-br from-amber-500 to-amber-700 text-white p-6 shadow-md">
<div class="text-3xl font-bold opacity-30 mb-1">02</div>
<div class="font-bold text-lg mb-2">Nothing you can't read</div>
<div class="text-sm opacity-90">If you cannot explain the line, it does not go in the file. "It ran" is not a review.</div>
</div>

<div class="rounded-xl bg-gradient-to-br from-emerald-500 to-emerald-700 text-white p-6 shadow-md">
<div class="text-3xl font-bold opacity-30 mb-1">03</div>
<div class="font-bold text-lg mb-2">You own the result</div>
<div class="text-sm opacity-90">Your name is on the paper, not the model's. It is a fast, confident colleague who has never seen your data.</div>
</div>

</div>

<div class="mt-10 text-center text-lg font-light">
Everything else today is <em>mechanics</em>.
